# Bay Area: A Seven-Day Trip

This was my first trip to the US, in early June 2025.

The Bay Area was not completely unfamiliar to me. I happened to know a few friends working there, and I had watched Silicon Valley before.

My classmate N happened to be working there, and he had not moved back to China yet, so I took the window and went.

This is the travel log.

## Day 0: Before the Trip

I used to think getting a US visa would be troublesome. I am from Fujian, and at the time I was working for a sanctioned company, so I was worried the interview might get stuck.

Later I got my HK ID and saw that US visa processing in Hong Kong was pretty efficient. So I filled out the DS-160, prepared the interview materials, and went for the interview. It was smoother than expected.

The main things I wanted to experience on this trip:

- American car culture
- Flying a plane
- Shooting range
- Driving along Highway 1
- Bay Area life

## Day 1: Arrival + Car Window Smashed

I arrived in the afternoon, and N came to pick me up at the airport.

It was still early, so we went to take a look at the Golden Gate Bridge first.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/golden-gate.jpg)

We took a couple of photos at the viewpoint. Less than five minutes away from the car, the window got smashed. Both bags on the back seat were gone, and both of our passports were inside. N had been in the US for years and somehow still had not learned this one. Some lessons only need to happen once.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/broken-window.jpg)

First day in the US, and I immediately unlocked the police report experience. A pretty direct taste of the local freedom.

By the time we finished reporting it, it was already dark. We got back to N's place pretty late and brought Popeyes fried chicken for dinner.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/popeyes.jpg)

## Day 2: Shooting Range + Stanford

The second day was shooting range.

The coach really knew how to give emotional support. It felt like getting a pentakill in a game. The moving target felt especially good.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/shooting-range.png)

When we went to Stanford, we had no idea there was a Coldplay concert that day. I accidentally paid $80 for parking, basically as an outside-the-venue ticket.

Also met a classmate who was doing a PhD at Stanford.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/stanford.jpg)

After that, we had In-N-Out as late-night food. Good. Very solid.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/in-n-out.jpg)

## Day 3: Highway 1

The third day was Highway 1, from Cupertino to Big Sur.

N drove. I did not have a driver's license, so my job was sitting in the car, looking at the view, and taking photos.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/highway-1-selfie.png)

In the evening, we had a crab tower in Monterey.

![](https://cdn.luohy15.com/images/bay-area/monterey-crab-tower.jpg)

The whole day felt a bit like walking inside a macOS version. Pretty fun.
